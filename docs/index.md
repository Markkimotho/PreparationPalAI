## A few tools. One learning loop.

Start with [Preparation Coach](features/coach.md) when you want a structured session.
Use [Study Agent](features/tutor.md) when you want a conversation. Bring your own
[notes and documents](features/material.md), ask [source-backed questions](features/chat.md),
or build [flashcards](features/flashcards.md) for active recall.

You choose the topic and a local model. PreparationPalAI helps you practise;
your course material and your own reasoning remain the reference.
