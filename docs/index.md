# User guide

PreparationPalAI generates practice questions, explains topics, and answers questions
from your notes using local models. This guide covers the app’s controls and study workflows.

New to the app? [Run your first study session](start/first-session.md).
If you haven’t installed it or joined a workspace, [start with access and installation](start/access.md).

## Study workflows

- [Prepare for an exam](playbooks/exams.md) — choose a syllabus topic, practise without a timer, then try a timed session.
- [Practise a technical interview](playbooks/interviews.md) — answer technical questions and work through follow-ups.
- [Recap a topic](playbooks/recap.md) — test what you remember and review what you missed.

## Use the tools

| Tool | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| [Preparation Coach](features/coach.md) | Start a saved exam, interview, or recap session and review your results. |
| [Study material](features/material.md) | Upload PDFs or images and check that they are ready to use. |
| [Quiz Prep](features/quizzes.md) | Generate a quiz from documents or a topic. |
| [Study Agent](features/tutor.md) | Ask for explanations, examples, and follow-up questions. |
| [Document chat](features/chat.md) | Ask questions about your uploaded material and inspect the sources. |
| [Flashcards](features/flashcards.md) | Create a deck and test your recall. |
| [Programming Assistant](features/code.md) | Discuss code, algorithms, and debugging. |

## Models and help

[Choose a model](features/models.md) covers GLM, DeepSeek, and the model picker.
For connection, upload, or generation failures, see [troubleshooting](help/troubleshooting.md).
Read [privacy and workspaces](help/privacy.md) for storage and access details, or
[questions and limitations](help/faq.md) for what the app currently supports.

This guide describes the latest development version. Your installation may have
fewer options. Generated answers and scores can be wrong; check them against your course material.
