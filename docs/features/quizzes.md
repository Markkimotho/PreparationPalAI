# Quiz Prep

Use Quiz Prep for a straightforward quiz from your indexed material. For a saved
session, an exam timer, or practice without documents, use
[Preparation Coach](coach.md) instead.

## Create a quiz

1. [Add relevant material](material.md) and wait for indexing to finish.
2. Open **Quiz Prep** and enter the topic you want to test.
3. Choose **Multiple Choice**, **True / False**, **Short Answer**, or **Mixed**.
4. Set the question count, difficulty, and exam type.
5. Select **Generate quiz**.

The exam-type choice helps shape the questions; it is not an official exam board’s
question bank or marking scheme.

## Work through the questions

Choose an option or write an answer, then select **Submit answer**. Review the
feedback, expand the explanation if useful, and continue to the next question.

For a short answer, explain the concept in your own words. Copying the reference
may raise a score without improving your recall.

## Use the results

At the end, examine the question breakdown. For each mistake, identify whether
you missed a fact, misunderstood a relationship, or applied the wrong method.

- **Retake same quiz** is useful for checking that you understood the feedback.
- **New quiz** gives another opportunity to practise the topic with different questions.
- **Study Agent** can help explain a difficult concept in a conversation.

For a longer-term record of weak topics, complete your practice in Preparation Coach.
Quiz Prep’s current quiz state can be lost when you leave or refresh the page.
Use [Download study packs](exports.md) to keep a fillable exam, worksheet, revision
checklist, or separate answer key before leaving.

!!! question "No questions were generated?"
    Check that indexing has completed and that the selected material actually covers
    the topic. Try a narrower topic, fewer questions, or another available model.
