# Preparation Coach

Preparation Coach is the place for a structured study session. Choose a topic,
answer one question at a time, and finish with results you can revisit.

## Choose a mode

| Mode | What you’ll do | A good use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Learn & recap** | Test understanding and get a targeted question after a misconception when the model can generate one | Revisit a chapter or refresh a foundation |
| **Exam practice** | Take an assessment with your chosen format and an optional time limit | Rehearse answering under pressure |
| **Technical interview** | Write out your reasoning and respond to a model-generated follow-up | Practise explaining an approach or defending an assumption |
| **Weak-area review** | Prioritise topics where earlier saved answers scored poorly | Decide what to revise next |

## Set up the session

1. Enter a specific **topic** or syllabus focus.
2. Select the **difficulty** and **question count**.
3. Choose a **question format** where available. Technical Interview uses written
   responses. Mixed format in Exam Practice asks for more than one question style.
4. In Exam Practice, choose **Untimed** or a time limit.
5. Select **Start preparation** and allow the selected local model to generate questions.

The model can reject a request if it produces malformed questions. Retrying with
fewer questions or another model is better than practising from broken output.

## Answer and review

For written answers, show your method. In science and mathematics, include the
assumptions, units, intermediate steps, and interpretation of the result.

Use **Confidence** to record how certain you felt. A confident wrong answer is
especially useful to revisit: it may reveal a misconception rather than a memory gap.

After **Submit answer**, read the feedback and expand **Reference answer and solution**.
When returned by the model, the written-answer rubric separates:

- **Reasoning:** up to 40 points.
- **Accuracy and units:** up to 40 points.
- **Clarity and completeness:** up to 20 points.

Multiple-choice and true/false answers are checked against the generated reference.
Written answers are judged by the model. Neither the reference nor the grading is
guaranteed correct; use your notes to resolve a disagreement.

## Read the results

**View results** shows the session score, topic breakdown, missed concepts, and
recommended next actions. Unanswered questions count as zero in the overall score.

Choose **Practise weak areas** to create a review session. When saved results
exist, review mode can prioritise those weak topics instead of the topic you typed.
With no saved results yet, it uses your chosen topic.

## Timers and saved sessions

An exam timer starts after the questions have been generated. It continues while
you are away from the page. Once time runs out, the session moves to results;
refreshing does not restart its timer.

Use **Your recent sessions** to resume or revisit a saved session. Interview
follow-ups can add a question, so the total may grow during an interview session.

!!! note "Source-backed or general knowledge?"
    With retrieved material, the coach lists contributing sources. Without material,
    it labels the session **General model knowledge**. A source label makes an answer
    easier to check; it does not certify that every generated statement is correct.
