# Practise a technical interview

A technical interview tests more than recalling a fact. Use practice to make your
reasoning understandable, expose assumptions, and respond when the problem changes.

## Define the interview you want

In **Preparation Coach**, choose **Technical interview** and describe the field,
level, and focus in the topic box.

For example:

> Graduate research interview: experimental design, uncertainty, and interpreting
> anomalous measurements in materials science.

> Technical software interview: binary search invariants, boundary cases, and
> complexity trade-offs.

> Engineering interview: conservation laws, order-of-magnitude estimates, and
> explaining modelling assumptions.

Upload a course summary, role-relevant notes, or a specification if you want
source-backed practice. Without material, the session is labelled general model knowledge.

## Answer as though someone is listening

Write an answer with four parts where appropriate:

1. **Clarify:** what is being asked, and what information is missing?
2. **Assume:** which conditions or simplifications are you using?
3. **Reason:** show the method, derivation, or trade-off.
4. **Check:** test units, edge cases, plausibility, or limitations.

You can rehearse aloud before typing your response, but the app currently grades
the written answer. It is not a voice interviewer.

## Take the follow-up seriously

The coach can add a follow-up based on your answer. Treat it as an opportunity to
explain why your method applies, not as a signal that your first answer was wrong.
The question total can increase when a follow-up is added.

If the follow-up or feedback seems technically unsound, verify it against a trusted
reference. Local model quality varies, especially on specialised topics.

## Finish with one improvement target

Use the results to identify a specific habit to practise next: clearer assumptions,
more precise terminology, a missing unit check, or a better explanation of a trade-off.

For open-ended follow-up practice, switch to [Study Agent](../features/tutor.md).
For implementation details and code review, use [Programming Assistant](../features/code.md).

!!! note "Practice, not a prediction"
    Generated questions are rehearsal material. They are not inside knowledge of a
    company’s interview, a guarantee of coverage, or a prediction of your result.
