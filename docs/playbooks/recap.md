# Recap a topic

Use a small session when you want to refresh a topic without turning it into a
full exam. This is useful before a lecture, after reading a chapter, or when you
return to a subject after a break.

## Begin with a question, not a long summary

Write down what you think the concept means before opening the reference. Then
choose **Learn & recap** in Preparation Coach with a narrow topic and a few questions.

> Conditional probability: distinguish P(A given B) from P(B given A), with a small example.

> Explain why conservation of energy does not mean kinetic energy stays constant.

If you need a first explanation rather than assessment, ask Study Agent to explain
the idea, then return to the coach for recall practice.

## Look for the missing connection

After each answer, ask yourself:

- Can I explain the concept without repeating the reference word for word?
- Do I know when it applies and when it does not?
- Can I give an example or a counterexample?
- Which assumption would change the result?

Recap mode may adapt a question to a concept you missed. If you keep struggling,
reduce the difficulty or choose a smaller part of the topic.

## Keep only the useful reminders

Make a short flashcard deck for the ideas you actually had trouble recalling.
Use one prompt per card. Return later and answer before flipping; rereading a deck
is not the same task as retrieving an answer.

Saved coach results help **Weak-area review** prioritise future practice. Flashcards
do not schedule reminders automatically, so pick your own time to revisit them.
